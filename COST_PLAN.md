## Executive Summary
This document outlines the professional cost plan and pricing structure for The Modern Pedagogues' full-stack organizational website. The platform is a production-grade system that supports marketing, sales, and operational workflows. The pricing reflects Ghana-based affordability while aligning with international professional standards.

## 1) Initial Project Value (Completed System)
**Total System Value:** **GHS 60,000**

**Justification:**
- End-to-end architecture, development, and deployment
- Integrated e-commerce flow for books and resources
- Operational admin workflows and content management
- Scalable foundation for MoMo and Visa payment integration
- Professional design, testing, and production readiness

This figure represents the asset value of the completed system and is aligned with agency-level project pricing for an equivalent scope.

## 2) Monthly Maintenance Fee
**Standard Maintenance Plan:** **GHS 2,500 / month**

## 3) What Monthly Maintenance Covers
Included each month:
- Hosting management (Heroku configuration and oversight)
- Server monitoring and uptime checks
- Content updates (text, images, and minor page edits)
- Feature updates within the existing scope
- Security patches and dependency updates
- Backup and restore readiness
- Technical support for operational issues

**Why this costs GHS 2,500/month:**
This replaces the need for a dedicated technical hire, while ensuring continuous platform stability, proactive maintenance, and timely updates.

## 4) Future Pricing Tiers (Scaling with Workload)
**Tier 1 - Standard (GHS 2,500/month)**
- Steady usage and light updates

**Tier 2 - Growth (GHS 4,500/month)**
- Increased content volume
- More frequent feature improvements
- Higher support workload

**Tier 3 - Expansion (GHS 7,500/month)**
- High traffic or complex operations
- Regular feature additions
- Priority support and faster turnaround

**Justification:**
As usage grows, so do infrastructure needs, update frequency, and operational risk. These tiers scale coverage and responsiveness accordingly.

## 5) Optional Extra Charges (Major Enhancements)
These items are outside standard maintenance and are scoped separately:
- Major new feature module: **GHS 8,000 - 25,000**
- Full redesign or UI overhaul: **GHS 15,000 - 35,000**
- MoMo and Visa payment integration: **GHS 10,000 - 20,000**
- New e-commerce functionality: **GHS 8,000 - 18,000**
- Advanced analytics or reporting dashboards: **GHS 7,000 - 15,000**

## 6) Why This Is Cheaper Than Hiring Full-Time
A competent full-time developer in Ghana can cost **GHS 10,000 - 20,000 per month**, excluding:
- Hardware and software tools
- Employee benefits
- Management overhead
- Leave, training, and downtime

This plan delivers professional coverage at a fraction of full-time staffing cost while retaining full technical ownership and accountability.

## 7) Recommended Plan Summary
- System Value: **GHS 60,000**
- Monthly Maintenance: **GHS 2,500**
- Scalable Tiers: **GHS 4,500 / GHS 7,500**
- Major Enhancements: scoped separately

## Payment Terms
- Monthly maintenance is billed in advance on the 1st of each month.
- Major enhancements are billed 50 percent upfront and 50 percent on delivery.
- Any third-party fees (payment gateways, external services) are billed separately.
